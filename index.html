Questions, answered.

Is ZED Prepare AI free to use?

Yes. Students can get started with core learning, community, and practice tools for free. Premium adds unlimited practice and deeper personal insights, while schools receive tailored plans.

Which curriculums are supported?

100+ curriculum pathways across 50+ countries, including WAEC, NECO, GCSE, A-Levels, Common Core, AP, CBSE, ICSE and more.

Can my school use ZED Prepare AI?

Yes. Schools get a teacher and admin dashboard, class management, bulk student accounts and dedicated support. Contact schools@zedprepare.ai.

How do students get started?

Choose a country, curriculum, grade and subjects, and ZED builds a personalized study path in under a minute.
